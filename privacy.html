Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Lauren is a private, single-user personal assistant. It is operated by its owner for his own use and is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in, Lauren accesses the owner's own Gmail messages and drafts and Google Calendar events. It accesses no other user's data.

How the data is used

  • To summarize and organize the owner's email, prepare reply drafts for the owner's review, track purchases and deliveries, and manage the owner's calendar.
  • Nothing is sent, archived or changed without the owner's approval, except under rules the owner has explicitly enabled.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Portions of email content may be processed by an AI model provider solely to perform the functions above for the owner.

Storage and security

Data is stored in the owner's private cloud account, with access limited to the owner. Credentials are kept in a managed secret store.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Lauren's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Lauren's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
